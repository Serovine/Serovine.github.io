Makruk Thai (Thai Chess) — Quick Rules

Makruk is the traditional chess of Thailand, believed to be one of the closest living relatives to the original Indian Chaturanga. You play as Ivory White (moving first from the bottom). The CPU plays as Onyx Black on Easy/Normal and switches to Crimson Red on Hard!

Piece Movements

Special Rules to Know

  • Starting Position: Pawns (Bia) start on the 3rd rank (row), not the 2nd! Look closely too: the White King starts on the right of his Met, while the CPU's Met stands opposite your King.
  • Pawn Promotion (Bia Ngai): When a Pawn reaches the 6th rank (the opponent's pawn starting line), it flips over immediately into a Promoted Pawn (Bia Ngai) and moves like a Met (1 step diagonally).
  • No Castling or Double-Step: Pawns always move 1 square forward (no 2-square initial jump and no en passant). Kings cannot castle.
  • 64-Move Draw Countdown: If 64 consecutive moves (half-moves) occur without any piece being captured, the game automatically ends in a Draw.
  • Victory: Checkmate (Ruk Jon) wins the game. Stalemate (Ab) results in a Draw.
