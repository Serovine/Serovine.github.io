⚔ ISEKAI TAVERN SIMULATOR
Run a tavern. Hire heroes. Send them off. Watch the gold roll in.
📖 How to Play▶
  • Pick a Quest from the board, then click Adventurers on the left to assemble a party.
  • Check the Success Rate bar before sending — higher is safer. Hit "Send Party" to commit gold and start the mission.
  • Quests resolve automatically after their duration. Watch the On Mission panel on the right for progress.
  • You can Reject quests you don't like — but rejecting too many hurts your Reputation.
🧙 The Five Classes▶
  • ⚔️ Warrior — high ATK & DEF, the frontline tank.
  • ✨ Mage — strong ATK, fragile, great for tough quests.
  • 💚 Healer — boosts party survival, often required for risky jobs.
  • 🏹 Scout — fast, balanced, raises success rate.
  • 🎒 Backpacker — weak in combat, but multiplies loot earnings.
🏰 Running the Tavern▶
  • Gold comes in automatically from Inn stays, food orders, and equipment shop commissions on materials your adventurers bring back.
  • Recruit new adventurers by waiting, posting a notice (pick a class), or headhunting a veteran from a rival tavern.
  • If your roster is full, recruiting a new hire may bench an idle adventurer to make room.
  • Spend gold to upgrade your Tavern — raises your adventurer cap and daily income (requires enough Reputation too).
  • Top performers appear on the 🏆 Board — spend gold to rank them up (★ up to 7) for a random stat boost.
  • Watch your gold — going too far into debt ends the run. Good luck, tavern keeper!
